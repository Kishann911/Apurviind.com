FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of stainless steel pipes does Apurvi Industries manufacture?

Apurvi Industries manufactures a comprehensive range including Pressure Pump Sleeves / Motor Shell Tubing, Motor Tubes, NRV Pipes / Pump Pipes, Aluminum Vapour Tubing, Higher HP Pump Tubes, and Manufacturing Long Tubes. All products are available in SS 304, SS 316, SS 202, and other grades as per customer specification.

Which industries does Apurvi Industries supply stainless steel pipes to?

We supply precision stainless steel components to 8+ industries including submersible pump manufacturing, motor manufacturing, fueling industry, water treatment, agriculture, food processing, chemical processing, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.

Does Apurvi Industries export stainless steel pipes internationally?

Yes, Apurvi Industries exports to 10+ countries including USA, Canada, Mexico, Germany, Italy, Turkey, Philippines, and Australia. We have established export relationships across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East.

Where is Apurvi Industries manufacturing plant located?
