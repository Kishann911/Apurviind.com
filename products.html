Product Specifications & Applications

Apurvi Industries manufactures stainless steel and aluminum piping products to international quality standards. Every product is built to exact customer tolerances using SS 304, SS 316, SS 202 grades, with options for bright-annealed, 2B, and mirror-polished finishes.

Stainless Steel Pressure Pump Sleeve / Motor Shell Tubing

Apurvi Industries manufactures precision stainless steel pressure pump sleeves and motor shell tubing for submersible pump and industrial motor applications. Our pump sleeves are produced from SS 304 and SS 316 grade material with ground OD and ID to tight tolerances, ensuring perfect assembly fit and extended operational life.

ParameterSpecification
Material GradeSS 304, SS 316, SS 202 (as per requirement)
Manufacturing StandardIS:6761, ASTM A268, EN 10296
Diameter Range2 inch to 12 inch OD (custom sizes available)
Surface FinishBright Annealed, 2B, Mirror Polished
LengthUp to 6 meters (custom lengths on request)
TolerancePrecision ground to exact customer specification

Applications

  • Submersible borewell pump sleeves — 0.5 HP to 200 HP
  • Industrial pump motor casings and shells
  • Agricultural submersible pump sleeves
  • Water-supply pump motor tubes
