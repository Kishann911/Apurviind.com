Our Leadership

Founder

Shri Viren Gathani

Managing Director

Under the visionary leadership of Shri Viren Gathani, Apurvi Industries has grown from a specialized manufacturing unit into a trusted national supplier of stainless steel pipe solutions. With over three decades of engineering expertise, Shri Gathani has been the driving force behind the company's commitment to quality and technical innovation.

His philosophy of "getting the right material from the right source at the right time" has become the foundation of our operational excellence. By focusing on vertically integrated production and a client-centric approach, he has navigated the company through decades of industrial shifts while maintaining a reputation for absolute integrity.

"Our success is built on the trust our clients place in our products. In the world of industrial manufacturing, precision is not just a goal—it is a responsibility."

Shri Gathani continues to oversee the strategic direction of the company, focusing on expanding our manufacturing capabilities and adopting sustainable production practices to serve the evolving needs of global industries.
