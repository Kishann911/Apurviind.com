Industry News & Events

April 2026 · Birmingham, UK

Apurvi Industries at the UK Metals Expo

Our team represented Apurvi Industries at the UK Metals Expo, the country's flagship gathering for the metals supply chain. We presented our full range of precision stainless steel pump tubes, motor tubes, and Nech ring stage pipes to engineers, OEMs, and distributors from across Europe.

Beyond the booth, the expo opened conversations with new partners exploring custom alloys for high-pressure submersible applications — a strong signal of growing demand for our Indian-engineered stainless steel components in the European market.

April 2026 · Dubai, UAE

Showcasing at the Dubai Industrial Exhibition

Apurvi Industries took its precision stainless steel portfolio to Dubai, joining manufacturers, water-management leaders, and pump OEMs from across the Middle East and Africa. We highlighted our submersible pump tubes and motor housings engineered for the harsh, high-temperature operating conditions of the region.

The response reaffirmed Apurvi's growing footprint in the GCC market, with several MEA distributors initiating discussions on long-term supply partnerships for the 2026–2027 cycle.
